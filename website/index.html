Seal

Live captions of conversation around you, on your iPhone, with no network.

Follow conversation at your own pace

Pull out your iPhone. Tap once. Place it on the table. Read live, scrolling captions as people speak nearby — with speaker labels so you know who's talking — all without sending audio anywhere.

Built for people who follow conversation differently

Deaf and hard of hearing

Seal puts real-time captions in your hands so you can follow group conversation at the table, in meetings, and anywhere people are talking — without a service connection or a hearing aid.

People talking with them

You see exactly what's being said and who's saying it, in real time, on one screen. No guessing, no repeating. The speaker labels mean everyone can follow together.

Anyone in a noisy room

Background noise is shown dimmed, not deleted — so nothing's lost, but you can focus on what's closest. Seal never silently drops a word.

Your conversation stays on your phone

  • No cloud: everything happens on-device. Your audio never leaves your iPhone.
  • No accounts: Seal works out of the box with no sign-up, login, or authentication.
  • No collection: Seal collects nothing about you, your speech, or who you talk to.
  • No telemetry: there is no analytics, tracking, or crash reporting.

Seal uses Apple's SpeechAnalyzer (iOS 26+), which runs entirely on your device. The transcription model is provisioned by the system and may require a one-time download before first use, with zero network after that point.

How it works

  1. Listen

    Tap Start. Seal listens to the microphone and captures the audio around you.

  2. Transcribe and label

    Apple's speech engine converts audio to words in real time. A speaker-diarization model identifies who is speaking — Speaker 1, Speaker 2, etc. — with no training or enrollment needed.

  3. Display

    Words are paired with speakers, grouped at pauses and speaker changes, and shown in the live caption stream. The newest captions scroll into view automatically. You can customize the text size, color, background, and typeface.

Questions?

Does it work offline?
Yes. After the speech model is installed (a one-time download), Seal works completely offline. Nothing is sent to any server.
How many speakers can it label?
Seal can distinguish up to four speakers in a conversation, labeling each one as Speaker 1, Speaker 2, etc.
Can I customize the captions?
Yes. You can change the text size (following your system setting), text color, background color, and font. Tap Settings (gear icon) to adjust.
What languages does it support?
Seal currently works in English. The app automatically uses your system language setting for the interface, and can transcribe English at multiple regional accents (US, UK, Australian, etc.) — just pick your region in Settings.
Does it need microphone permission?
Yes. Seal asks for microphone access when you start your first captioning session. This is how it listens to conversation around you.
What if I'm in a very noisy place?
Seal shows low-confidence captions dimmed so you can focus on the clearest speech. Nothing is silently deleted — you decide what to read.
Do you collect my data?
No. Seal doesn't create an account, doesn't send your audio or captions anywhere, and doesn't track anything about you.
