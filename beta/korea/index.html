Testing Seal Speak in Korea

Thanks for trying this! Seal Speak shows what the people around you are saying as live captions on your iPhone. This test version can also translate those captions into English, on screen or spoken in your ear. Once it's set up, it all runs on the phone with no internet.

You need an iPhone 12 or newer on iOS 26, and headphones or AirPods for the in-ear part.

1. Install (2 minutes)

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. On your iPhone, tap the button below, then Accept and Install.

Get Seal Speak on TestFlight

In TestFlight, check that the version reads 1.1.0 (10). If it shows an older build, wait until Gil says build 10 is ready, then tap Update.

2. Set up before you go out (on Wi-Fi, 5–10 minutes)

The language downloads happen here, once. After that everything works offline.

  1. Open Seal Speak, and allow the microphone and speech recognition when it asks.
  2. Tap the gear to open Settings.
  3. Caption language: choose Korean (한국어). This is the language people around you speak. Let it finish downloading.
  4. Translate captions: tap Unlock. It's free in TestFlight, so you won't be charged. If iOS asks you to confirm a test purchase, that's normal.
  5. Translate into: choose English, and let the language pack finish downloading.
  6. Optional: turn on Speak it in my ear, and connect your headphones.
  7. Go back and tap Start captions. While you're still on Wi-Fi, check that it shows both Korean and English.

3. What to try

Anything you can, in any order.

  • 1. Korean captions. Someone talks to you in Korean: a shop, a taxi, a restaurant. Korean appears as they speak; words show faint at first, then firm up.
  • 2. Translation. The same, with Translate captions on. English appears within a second or two.
  • 3. In your ear. With headphones on and Speak it in my ear on, you hear the English quietly as people talk. It never plays out loud from the phone.
  • 4. Offline. Turn on Airplane Mode and try 1–3 again. Everything still works.
  • 5. Busy places. A market, the subway, a café. Notice what it misses.
  • 6. Two or more people. A new line, labelled Speaker 1 or Speaker 2, starts when the voice changes.
  • 7. Mixed languages. Someone switches between Korean and English, and it keeps up with both.
  • 8. Sounds. Laughter, applause, a doorbell or a knock get short labels between the lines.
  • 9. Saving. Tap ✕ to pause, then Save. The conversation stays on your phone for 30 days. Open it from the list icon, then search or share it.
  • 10. A long session. About 30 minutes. Say whether the phone gets hot or the battery drops a lot.

4. Tell Gil how it went

For each thing you tried, a quick worked / didn't work / weird, plus:

  • where you were and how noisy it was;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • a screenshot if something looks wrong. Take a screenshot in the app and TestFlight will offer Share Beta Feedback, which sends it straight to Gil. Or just text him.

Especially useful: Korean words it got wrong, translations that read badly, and anything that froze, stopped or sat on a silent screen.

Privacy: audio never leaves the phone. Nothing is recorded or uploaded unless you tap Save, and saved conversations stay on your phone.
