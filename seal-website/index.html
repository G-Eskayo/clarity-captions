Seal

Live captions for conversation, right on your phone.

What Seal Does

Captions

Live, scrolling transcript of what people are saying nearby, updated as you listen.

Speaker Labels

Know who's speaking. Each speaker gets a label — Speaker 1, Speaker 2 — so you follow the conversation.

Sound Labels

Don't miss ambient sounds. [Doorbell], [Laughter], [Car horn] — sound events appear in the caption stream alongside speech.

Adjustable Display

Make captions work for you. Change text size, color, background, and font independently.

Dim, Don't Drop

Background speech is shown dimmed, not hidden. You see what the app heard, even if it's faint.

Simple to Use

One obvious action. Open, tap Start, read captions. No setup, no training, no technical choices.

Built for Everyone

Seal is designed for people who are deaf or hard of hearing, and anyone else who benefits from live captions — whether you're in a loud restaurant, a meeting with accents you're adjusting to, or just want to follow conversation while doing something else.

No per-user training. No enrollment. Same behavior for every install, out of the box.

Your Privacy, On Your Phone

Nothing leaves your phone during captioning.

The entire captioning path — audio capture, transcription, speaker labeling — runs locally on your iPhone. No network call in that path at runtime. Ever.

One exception, stated precisely: the operating system may download its speech model on first use. That's a one-time fetch from Apple, not from us. After that model is installed, the app works offline.

How to Use Seal

  1. Open Seal

    Launch the app. That's it — no setup required.

  2. Tap Start

    Give microphone permission on first use, then start a captioning session.

  3. Read Captions

    Watch the live transcript appear as people speak. Tap Settings to adjust text size, colors, and font.

Captioning runs until you tap Stop, or until idle time passes (adjustable: 5, 15, 30 minutes, or never).
