Getting Started

When you open Seal, the app starts listening immediately. Captions appear as people speak. If you need to adjust text size, colors, or other settings, tap Settings and choose what works best for you.

Microphone Access

Seal needs access to your microphone to caption conversations. If captions aren't appearing:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Scroll down and tap "Seal"
  3. Make sure "Microphone" is turned on
  4. Return to the app and try again

No Audio or Captions Not Showing

If the app says "Can't hear":

  • Check that you haven't muted your phone (toggle the mute switch on the side)
  • Make sure the microphone isn't blocked or covered
  • Try moving your phone closer to whoever is speaking
  • If using speaker phone, keep it away from other noise

If captions still don't appear after a few seconds of speech, the audio may be too quiet or too far away. Seal works best with people speaking at a normal volume nearby.

Saving Conversations

Saved conversations are stored locally on your phone and automatically deleted after 30 days. You can delete them manually anytime from the app. Saved conversations never leave your device and are never backed up to iCloud.

Language and Accents

Seal uses Apple's speech recognition, which supports multiple languages and accents. The app learns to recognize whoever is speaking, but works best with clear speech at a normal volume.

Accessibility

Seal is designed for people who are deaf or hard of hearing. We've made it easy to adjust text size, colors, and contrast to match your needs. If something isn't accessible or could be improved, please let us know.

Reporting Issues

Found a bug or have a suggestion? Contact us at:

support@seal.app

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • What you were doing when the issue happened
  • What you expected to see
  • What happened instead
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About)

Privacy & Your Data

Seal runs entirely on your device. Your conversations are never sent anywhere. See our privacy policy for full details.

App Updates

New versions of Seal are released through the App Store. Open the App Store app on your iPhone and tap the Updates tab to install the latest version.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Seal cost anything?

No. Seal is free and will always be free. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or ads.

Is my microphone always on?

Only when you have the app open and actively captioning. The microphone turns off when you close Seal or stop captioning.

Can I use Seal on other devices?

Seal is available on iPhone only. We're exploring support for other Apple devices in the future.

Will Seal work offline?

Yes. Once the language model is downloaded on first use, Seal works completely offline. You don't need internet to use it.

How long are conversations saved?

Only conversations you save manually are stored on your phone, and they automatically delete after 30 days. Unsaved conversations disappear when you close the app.

Can I export my captions?

You can copy text from saved conversations within the app. We're working on additional export options for the future.
