Who It's For

Built for everyone who's part of a conversation.

Seal was created for a deaf person and the people talking with her. But a one-tap app that captions what's being said helps anyone in that spot: someone in a noisy cafe, a parent at a packed playground, anyone at dinner when you really want to hear what's being said.

No enrollment. No accounts. Just open, tap, and the conversation flows.

Your Privacy is the Design

Nothing you say ever leaves your phone.

  • No account — nothing to sign up for or log into
  • No servers — captions happen entirely on your device
  • No recordings — we never store your conversations
  • No tracking — we don't know how you use Seal
  • Your phone stays silent — we don't send your voice anywhere

How It Works

Three simple steps.

1

Open Seal

Tap the app icon on your home screen.

2

Tap to Caption

One big button says "Start." That's it. Your phone listens and captions everything.

3

Read Along

Captions scroll onto your screen as people talk. Adjust the look in Settings anytime.
