How it works

1

Open Seal

One large tap. That's all. The app starts listening immediately.

2

See who's speaking

Each person in the conversation is labeled — "Speaker 1", "Speaker 2" — so you know who said what.

3

Adjust as you like

If the text is too small, colors don't work for you, or you prefer a different look — tap Settings and choose what fits. Your preferences stick.

4

Keep talking

Everything continues in the background. The app handles interruptions, pauses, and restarts on its own.
