Live captions for conversations

Seal: caption stream, speaker labels, and sound recognition—all on your phone, always free.

What Seal does

Caption stream

Live, scrolling transcript of conversations happening around you, updated in real time as people speak.

Speaker labels

See who's talking right now with clear labels like "Speaker 1" and "Speaker 2"—no enrollment needed, just open and start.

Sound labels

Recognize ambient sounds—[Doorbell], [Laughter], [Keyboard]—so you catch every part of the conversation.

Built for everyone

Whether you're deaf, hard of hearing, learning a language, or in a noisy environment, Seal helps you follow conversations with confidence. No setup, no sign-up—just open the app and tap to start.

Your privacy, guaranteed

Everything runs on your phone. Audio never leaves your device. Captions are processed and stored locally. Nothing is recorded, nothing is sent anywhere.

Free, always. No subscriptions, no ads, no tracking. Seal is free to download and free to use, forever.

How it works

  1. 1

    Open Seal and tap Start

    One button, no configuration. The app begins listening to the conversation.

  2. 2

    Read live captions

    Watch the caption stream appear on your screen as people speak. Speaker labels show who's talking, and sound labels catch ambient sounds.

  3. 3

    Customize the look

    Adjust text size, colors, and font to suit your needs. Your preferences stay with you.
