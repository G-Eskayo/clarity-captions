Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Seal is a live captioning app that runs entirely on your device. We don't collect, store, or share any of your conversations or personal data. Your privacy is fundamental to how the app works.

What Seal Does

Seal captures audio from your device's microphone, transcribes it in real time on your phone, and displays the captions on your screen. All of this happens locally — nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share something.

Data We Collect

We collect no data. Seal does not:

  • Send your audio, transcriptions, or conversations to any server
  • Store your captions after you close the app (except locally for 30 days if you explicitly save a conversation)
  • Track your usage, location, or behavior
  • Use third-party analytics or advertising
  • Require an account or login
  • Request permission to access any data outside of your microphone

Language Model

Seal uses Apple's SpeechAnalyzer framework for on-device transcription. On first use, your device may download a language model asset from Apple. This is a one-time download; after that, all transcription runs locally with no further network access. The language model is a system-level resource shared with other Apple apps — Seal doesn't provide it, host it, or control what data Apple collects from its download.

Saved Conversations

If you save a conversation from the app, Seal stores the transcript locally on your phone. Saved transcripts:

  • Never leave your device
  • Are never backed up to iCloud or any cloud service
  • Automatically delete after 30 days
  • Can be manually deleted at any time

Third-Party Services

Seal is built with open-source software licensed under permissive licenses (Apache 2.0, CC BY 4.0). No third-party services, SDKs, or tracking libraries are included in the app.

Your Control

You have full control over your data:

  • Grant or deny microphone access in your phone's Settings
  • Save conversations you want to keep; unsaved ones vanish when you close the app
  • Manually delete saved conversations anytime
  • Uninstall the app to delete all local data

Children's Privacy

Seal does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Because we collect no data at all, this policy applies equally to users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to how Seal works in a way that affects your privacy, we'll update this policy and notify you in the app.
