# UI audit, 2026-10-10: everything Seal can show, and whether it was approved

**Why:** after testing v1 on his iPhone, the owner found screens he never approved and never saw in a PR (the seal
in a box after Start, a boxed "Seal tells voices apart" banner, an extra pop-up after [ Save ]). This audit lists
**every** screen, overlay, alert, sheet, banner, hint and transition the app can show, from the code
(`Apps/Spike/Sources`, the CaptionCore types that drive the UI) and from real simulator screenshots of `main` at
8454234 (iPhone 17 Pro, debug build; DEBUG-only demo flags reach each state, see `DemoMode.swift`).

**Approved** means it appears in an approved mock-up or in the images of a PR the owner approved, cited. Everything
else is **not approved** and gets its own question in the round-2 PR's Decisions. Approved items that break the
round-2 rules below (one screen, fades only, no boxes or borders) are also flagged.

Round-2 mock-ups: [`docs/design/mocks/round2/`](mocks/round2/). Rules: [`v1-polish-spec.md` § Round 2](v1-polish-spec.md#round-2-2026-10-10-one-screen-fades-only).

## The extra pop-up after [ Save ] (reported by the owner)

Reproduced. It is the **how-to-use tour**, not a save prompt. While the tour is on step 4 ("Tap [ Save ] to keep
it"), tapping [ Save ] calls `tour.did(.tappedSave)` (`ContentView.swift`, `CaptionModel.save()`), which advances
the tour immediately: within a fraction of a second the veil and [ Save ] / [ New ] are dimmed again and step 5's
bordered card ("Hold on words to copy them", with a spotlight frame on a caption) pops up, so the
[ ✔ ] → [ Saved ] moment is covered. Outside the tour, saving shows only [ ✔ ] → [ Saved ] (checked in a beta-mode
build: no rating card or prompt on Save; the beta rating card only follows [ New ]).
