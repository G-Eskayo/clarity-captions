Generated by `scripts/engine-eval` (`report-hebrew`) for #201 "[E] Hebrew: measure Apple's dictation engine against the open Hebrew models". Do not edit by hand. Method: [hebrew-accuracy-design.md](hebrew-accuracy-design.md).

Generated 2026-10-29 19:33 UTC. Every engine ran locally on the M4 Mac mini; nothing was sent to a cloud service.

WER = (S + D + I) / N over words, CER the same over characters (single spaces included), pooled over clips, after the #202 Hebrew normalizer on both sides (niqqud, maqaf, geresh, numbers as digits, fillers). **Lenient** also folds full and defective spelling (שולחן = שלחן); it sits beside the strict score, never instead of it. 95% confidence intervals: bootstrap over sentences (1,000 resamples). Only clips every engine finished are compared within a set. CER is the in-house decision metric: one wrong prefix letter (ו, ה, ב, ל, מ, ש, כ) costs a whole word in WER.
