# Language accuracy comparison (FLEURS)

Generated by `scripts/engine-eval` (`report-languages`) for #158 (Spanish, French, German, with the Android builds) and #234 (every other language both Parakeet and Apple caption). Do not edit by hand. Method: [language-accuracy-design.md](language-accuracy-design.md).

**Languages:** Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese · 2026-10-30 09:19 UTC.

WER = (S + D + I) / N, pooled over clips, after a per-language normalizer that keeps accents (an engine that writes *esta* for *está* makes an error). **Accent-blind** is the same score with accent marks removed, to show how much of the error is only accents. 95% confidence intervals: bootstrap over FLEURS sentences (1,000 resamples). Only clips every engine finished are compared. Targets (docs/testing/test-plan.md): **15% or less is good, 30% or less is acceptable**.

**FLEURS is read speech in quiet rooms**, not conversation. Every engine scores far better here than in a sit-down chat (#145 on EdAcc), so the ranking is the useful part and the absolute numbers flatter all of them. Mac numbers (M4 Mac mini, nice +15 next to other work), not iPhone numbers.
