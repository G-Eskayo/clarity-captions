Live captions for real conversations, right on your phone.

What Seal does

Live captions

Real-time captions of everything being said around you, as it happens. The app updates continuously while you're in a conversation.

Speaker labels

See who's talking. Each speaker is labeled ("Speaker 1", "Speaker 2") so you can follow the flow of conversation.

Sound labels

Background sounds are labeled too: "[Doorbell]", "[Laughter]", "[Phone ringing]". Never miss what's happening around you.

Simple controls

One tap to start. No setup. No settings to understand. Just open the app and start a conversation.

For anyone who needs live captions

Seal is built for anyone having conversations where live captions would help. That might be a restaurant, a family dinner, a meeting, or anywhere else people are talking.

No enrollment. No training. No account. Everyone gets the same experience, right out of the box.

Your privacy, guaranteed

On-device only

Everything happens on your phone. Audio, transcription, and captions never leave your device. No network required to caption.

No account

No sign-up. No login. No profile to create. Open the app, start captioning.

Free, forever

No cost now, no cost later. No ads, no subscriptions, no hidden charges.

How it works

  1. Open Seal. Grant microphone access the first time you start a session.
  2. Tap Start. The app begins listening and captioning right away.
  3. Read captions as they appear. Watch the screen as people speak.
  4. Stop whenever you're done. Tap Stop and your session is saved to your phone for 30 days.
