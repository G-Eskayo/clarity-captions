Seal

Live captions for any conversation. On your iPhone. On-device only.

What Seal Does

Point your iPhone at a conversation. Seal captions the live speech in real time with speaker labels, so you follow who says what. Background sounds are dimmed and labeled too, never dropped.

Live Caption Stream

Real-time captions as people speak nearby, with speaker labels (Speaker 1, Speaker 2, etc.) so you know who's talking.

Dim, Don't Dismiss

Background speech—far away, quiet, or hard to hear—shows up dimmed and grouped, never silently dropped. You decide what matters.

Sound Labels

Recognize ambient sounds like [Doorbell], [Laughter], or [Door opening] in the caption stream, in real time.

Built for Everyone

Seal is free and always will be. It's built for anyone who wants live captions in a conversation—whether that's a restaurant, a dinner table, a meeting, or a family gathering.

Usable without instructions: open the app, see captions. One tap to start, minimal buttons, and settings that are simple to adjust.

How It Works

  1. Point your phone at the conversation. Seal listens and starts captioning.
  2. See captions live with speaker labels, scrolling up as the conversation continues.
  3. Background speech is dimmed, labeled and grouped. You follow the main conversation easily.

On-Device Privacy Guarantee

Seal processes everything on your iPhone. No network call during captioning. No upload. No account. No tracking.

Seal's captioning engine (Apple's SpeechTranscriber) may download a language model on first use—a one-time system fetch. After that, the app works offline.

This is different from services like Otter, which require a live internet connection to caption at all.
