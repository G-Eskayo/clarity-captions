Seal — Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Seal is built with privacy as a core principle. This policy explains how Seal handles your data.

On-Device Processing

All captioning happens on your iPhone. We do not send your audio, captions, or any information about your conversations to our servers or third parties.

No Accounts or Sign-In

Seal does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information to use the app.

Language Model Download

The first time you use Seal, your iPhone may download Apple's speech recognition language model from Apple's servers. This is a one-time system-level operation, not initiated by Seal. After that download, Seal works offline.

No Tracking or Analytics

Seal does not include analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind. We do not collect data about your usage, your conversations, or your location.

Saved Conversations

Captions from each session are saved automatically to your iPhone and deleted after 30 days. These conversations are never backed up to iCloud, synced, or shared.

Third-Party Services

Seal does not use third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page. Your continued use of Seal after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions?

If you have questions about privacy in Seal, contact us at geskayo@gmail.com.
