Made for you.

  • No internet neededIt works at a restaurant, in the car, anywhere.

Get it on your iPhone.

I'll be with you on FaceTime while you do this.

  1. Get TestFlight. It's Apple's free app for trying new apps before they're in the App Store.
    Get TestFlight
  2. Get Seal Speak. Tap this, then tap Accept and Install.
    Get Seal Speak
  3. Open Seal Speak and tap Start captions. When it asks to use the microphone, tap Allow. Then set your phone on the table and talk.

It works on your computer too.

On a Mac, Seal Speak shows the captions in a small window that stays on top of everything else.
