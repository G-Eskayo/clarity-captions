Get Help with Seal

Getting Started

How do I start captioning? Open the app and tap the main button. Seal will start listening and show captions in real time.

What if I don't hear anything? The app will tell you if it can't hear audio ("I can't hear anything"). This usually means the microphone is covered or the room is very loud. Try moving your phone or uncovering the microphone.

Can I adjust the captions? Yes. Open Settings to change text size, color, background, and font. These settings are saved and apply every time you open the app.

Common Questions

Does Seal work offline? Yes. After the first time you open the app, Seal works completely offline. Your iPhone does not need an internet connection to caption conversations.

Will my conversations be saved? Yes. Each captioning session is automatically saved on your phone and can be reviewed later. Saved sessions are deleted after 30 days.

Is Seal free? Yes, Seal is completely free and always will be. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads.

What about privacy? Seal processes everything on your iPhone. We do not send your audio, captions, or any information to our servers or third parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How many speakers can Seal detect? Seal can distinguish up to 4 speakers in a conversation and label them as Speaker 1, Speaker 2, etc. In very noisy environments or with many people talking, accuracy may vary.

Can Seal recognize other sounds? Yes. Seal recognizes common ambient sounds like doorbells, laughter, applause, knocking, and phone ringing, and adds them to the caption stream as labels like [Doorbell].

Troubleshooting

The app keeps stopping. Seal will automatically pause captioning after 5 minutes of no speech. You can change this setting or turn it off in the app's Settings.

Captions are delayed. A small delay is normal as the app processes audio in real time. If the delay is very long, try closing other apps running in the background.

The app is crashing. Try closing Seal completely and reopening it. If crashes continue, please contact support.
