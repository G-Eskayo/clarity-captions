Questions

Does Seal need the internet?

Only once. The first time you start, your device may download Apple's speech files. After that, Seal captions with no internet connection, including in airplane mode.

Does Seal record or upload my conversations?

No. Sound is turned into text on your device and the audio is never kept. The text of each conversation is saved on your device for 30 days so you can reread it, then deleted. See the privacy policy.

What does Seal cost?

Nothing. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

Which devices and languages does it work with?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and Mac with macOS 26 or later. Captions are in English for now.

Why does it say "Speaker 1" and "Speaker 2"?

Seal notices when a different person starts talking and starts a new line for them. It does not know who people are. Tap a speaker label to give that voice a name for the conversation.

The captions got a name or word wrong.

Open Settings and add it under "Words and names". Seal will then expect those words. Captions are made by a computer and can contain mistakes, so for anything important, check with the person speaking.

The screen says "I can't hear anything".

The microphone is hearing almost nothing. Check that nothing is covering the bottom of the phone, and place the phone flat on the table between you and the people talking.

Captions stopped by themselves.

The screen will say why. Tap Start again to carry on. If it keeps happening, please email us.

How do I delete saved conversations?

Open saved conversations and swipe to delete one, or delete them all. They are also deleted automatically after 30 days, and when you delete the app.
